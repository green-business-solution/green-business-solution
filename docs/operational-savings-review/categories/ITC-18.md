# Information Card — Community Solar Subscription

**Retrofits included:** Community solar subscription

**Overview:** A community solar subscription changes the electric bill through contracted energy credits and subscription charges.

**Broader Formula**

```text
Annual Operational Savings =
Annual Bill Credits − Annual Subscription Charges
```

**Expanded Formula**

```text
Annual Operational Savings = Annual Bill Credits - Annual Subscription Charges

Annual Bill Credits = Sum Across Billing Periods of Credited Electricity by Period × Contract Credit Rate by Period
```

**Information Tree**

```text
Annual Operational Savings
├─ Allocated or credited kWh by period from the executed contract or statement (Linked Opportunity)
├─ Credit rate by period from the executed contract or statement (Linked Opportunity)
├─ Subscription charge by period from the executed contract or statement (Linked Opportunity)
├─ Contract escalation rule (Linked Opportunity)
└─ Contract term rule (Linked Opportunity)
```
